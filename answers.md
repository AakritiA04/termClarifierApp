AI-based application that converts textbook physics problems into animated visualizations. Students enter a question, and the application visually deconstructs vectors, forces, or motions—concretizing abstract concepts.

Feature 2: Smart Revision Bot
Chatbot that has been trained on the NCERT syllabus and questions students with specific questions from weak spots. It provides voice + visual explanation for answers and monitors day-to-day improvement.

User Journey:-

Student logs on to the app and chooses "Motion in a Straight Line".

Types a problem: "A car accelerates from rest at 2 m/s²."

Concept Visualizer displays an animation of a car with time-stamped velocity indicators.

After grasping, the student launches the Smart Revision Bot.

Bot fires MCQs and short answers, adjusts based on incorrect responses.

End-of-session summary lists areas to revise with recommended animations.
